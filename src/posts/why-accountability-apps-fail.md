## The Problem With Passive Blockers

Every accountability app makes the same mistake: they assume the problem is **access**.

Block the app. Set a timer. Make it harder to open Instagram.

But you've never once been stopped by a screen time limit you set yourself. You just tap "ignore limit" and move on. The app lets you. Because it has no stake in the outcome.

**A wall you built yourself is a wall you can tear down.**

## What Actually Drives Bad Habits

Research from the American Psychological Association is clear: habits aren't broken by friction alone. They're broken by **interrupting the reward loop at the moment of trigger**.

The trigger isn't your phone. The trigger is boredom at 11pm. Anxiety before a deadline. The specific emotional state that makes doom-scrolling feel like relief.

No blocker addresses that. They address the symptom, not the moment.

That's not a blocker. That's a sparring partner.
