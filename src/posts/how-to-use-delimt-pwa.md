# How to Use delimt on Your Phone (PWA Guide)

delimt is a PWA — a Progressive Web App. That means it works like a native app on your phone without going through the App Store or Play Store. Here's how to get it running in under 2 minutes.

---

## What Is a PWA?

A PWA is a website that behaves like an app. It installs on your home screen, runs fullscreen with no browser bar, works offline, and can send you push notifications.

delimt ships as a PWA first because it means you can start using it today — no waiting for App Store approvals, no 200MB download.

---

## Install on Android

1. Open **Chrome** on your Android device
2. Go to **delimt.com**
3. Tap the **three-dot menu** (top right)
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Tap **"Install"** on the prompt
6. delimt appears on your home screen like any other app

That's it. Tap it to open — it launches fullscreen with no browser chrome.

---

## Install on iPhone (iOS 16.4+)

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone
2. Go to **delimt.com**
3. Tap the **Share button** (the box with an arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Tap **"Add"** in the top right
6. delimt appears on your home screen

**Important:** Use Safari, not Chrome, on iPhone. iOS only allows PWA installation through Safari.

## How to Use It

**Step 1 — Create your account**

Go to delimt.com/auth and sign up. Use a real email — your welcome sequence and intervention history are tied to it.

**Step 2 — Add your first habit**

Tap a preset habit (Doom Scrolling, Impulse Buying, Texting Ex, Late Night Snacking, Procrastinating) or create a custom one.

When you add a habit, delimt asks you two questions:

- **Why do you want to stop?** Be honest. The more specific, the harder delimt fights.
- **What's your usual excuse?** The thing you tell yourself right before giving in.

delimt stores these and uses them against you in every intervention.

**Step 3 — Hit "I need help" when the urge strikes**

The moment you feel the pull — open delimt and tap **"I need help"** next to the relevant habit.

delimt intercepts you immediately with a personalised argument. Don't close the app. Argue back. That's the point.

**Step 4 — Tell delimt what happened**

After the intervention, tap either:
- **"I resisted"** — your streak grows, the win is logged
- **"I gave in"** — no judgment, it's logged, tomorrow you fight again

The history of both builds your self-awareness over time.

---

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of delimt

**Be ruthlessly honest when setting up your habit profile.** Generic answers get generic responses. If your real excuse is "I'm lonely at night" — write that. delimt will use it.

**Open delimt before you give in, not after.** The intervention only works if you catch yourself at the trigger moment. Make it a habit to open delimt the second you feel the urge — not 20 minutes into doom scrolling.

**Don't delete a habit when you have a bad day.** The streak resets but the history stays. Seeing your pattern over weeks is part of how this works.

**Set delimt as a widget or keep it on your first home screen page.** Friction is the enemy. The faster you can open delimt at the trigger moment, the better it works.
