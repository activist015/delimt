# We're Building delimt — Here's the Full Story

This started with a simple frustration.

---

## The Override Problem

Every screen time app I tried had the same fatal flaw: I built it, so I could break it.

Set a limit for Instagram. Hit the limit. Tap "ignore for today." Done. The app never pushed back. It just... accepted defeat on my behalf.

I didn't need a limit. I needed something that would **argue with me**.

---

## The Insight

The moment I stopped scrolling wasn't when an app blocked me. It was when a friend texted: *"Didn't you say you were trying to stop doing this?"*

A human. Who knew my context. Who caught me in the moment. Who made it awkward to continue.

That's what no app had ever done.

**delimt is that friend. Built in AI. Available at 1am.**

---

## What We're Building

delimt is an active AI accountability agent. You tell it:

- What habit you're fighting
- Why you want to stop
- What excuses you usually make

Then when you hit your trigger moment, delimt intercepts you with a personalised argument built around your specific psychology. Not a timer. Not a block. A conversation that makes you pause long enough to make a real decision.

---

## What's Coming

- **Web app** — the core intervention engine, launching first
- **Chrome Extension** — intercepts you on the actual page you're doom-scrolling
- **Mobile app** — for impulse buying, late-night texting, all the off-screen habits
